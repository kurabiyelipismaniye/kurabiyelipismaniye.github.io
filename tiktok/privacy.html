Privacy Policy – Kurabiyeli Pişmaniye Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-03

This tool is used only by the channel owner on their own computer. It requests the TikTok permissions user.info.basic and video.upload solely to upload the owner's own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts.

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is collected from other people, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions).
